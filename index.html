Initial Investigation

After reviewing the available data, I wanted to investigate the happiness score across each region to identify which regions were the happiest and which had the lowest levels of happiness.

Results

After filtering the data and grouping it by region, the results showed that Australia & New Zealand had the highest average happiness score at 7.32, while Sub-Saharan Africa had the lowest at 4.14.

Further Analysis

We can dive deeper into the data by using the happiness score as a reference point to investigate whether any of the other statistics are contributing to the differences in happiness between regions. For example, we could investigate whether there is a correlation between happiness and the average GDP per region, or between happiness and average trust in government.
